Exam Result Management System

Class

Students

Each subject has Internal and Theory mark boxes (each with its own max); the Total mark is calculated automatically. Type AB for absent. 0 and AB are excluded from every calculation.

Subjects

Set each subject's Internal and Theory max marks (e.g. 50 and 100). Total max = Internal + Theory.

Class Summary

Overall totals and averages for the whole class.
Average Mark = a student's total marks ÷ number of subjects. Class Average = sum of all students' average marks ÷ number of students. 0 and AB are excluded from every calculation.

Subject Statistics

Subject Average Mark = subject total ÷ students who wrote it. All figures are rounded to whole numbers. The last row is the overall total for the class.

Student Averages

Each student's average mark (total ÷ subjects), best first.

Subject Audit Summary

Tap a band to see which students fall in it. Bands are based on each subject's combined percentage.

🤖 AI Assistant

A built-in analyst for this class. It works offline — your data never leaves this device.
